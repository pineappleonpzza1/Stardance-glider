# Stardance-glider
I am building an RC glider, with a focus on efficiency and manufacturability. 
3D printed components:
Wing, tail and electronics housing will be designed based my simulations in XFLR5, designed in Fusion 360 and 3D printed from LWPLA.
Hardware:
The goal is to combine the majority of all components needed for eventual autonomous flight in a single PCB of my design. The flight controller will be based on the architecture of an existing ESP 32 Dev Board and combine a charging circuit for the use of Lipo batteries, an accelerometer and GPS.
One of the goals is to achieve comparable weight and surface finish to full carbon fiber gliders with much simpler methods of construction.
